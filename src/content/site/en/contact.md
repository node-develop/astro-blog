## Send me a message

The primary and most reliable contact channel is email:
[a@artka.dev](mailto:a@artka.dev). Messages are read by Artyom Kashuta, the author
of artka.dev. Please include the relevant article, course, or project URL and a
short description of the outcome you need. That context makes it much easier to
give a useful answer without a long discovery exchange.

## Good reasons to get in touch

You are welcome to report factual errors, suggest corrections or translations,
propose article topics, or ask about Claude Code, AI-agent architecture,
retrieval and evaluation pipelines, and production backend engineering.
Proposals for technical collaboration, talks, interviews, and engineering review
are also appropriate. To report a security issue, put “Security” in the subject
and avoid publishing sensitive details before receiving a response.

artka.dev is an independent technical publication, not official support for
Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or any other company mentioned in its articles. Account,
billing, quota, and product-availability questions should go to the relevant
vendor. Never send passwords, API keys, access tokens, private datasets, or other
secrets to this address.

## What happens to your message

I try to answer substantive messages, but there is no guaranteed response time
and unsolicited advertising or requests outside the site's scope may not receive
a reply. Message content is used to understand the enquiry and continue the
conversation. The [Privacy page](/en/privacy/) explains how this website handles
analytics, browser-side preferences, and third-party services.

Verified public profiles and author links are listed on the
[About page](/en/about/). When citing site content, use the canonical URL of the
specific page and attribute it to “Artyom Kashuta” (“Артём Кашута”).
