## Who I Am

Artem Kashuta. 10+ years in development: backend, infrastructure, recent years — agentic systems and LLM tooling. Remote-based. I write in Russian, read and work in English.

## What I Do

- **AI agent engineering** — harness/agent loop, tool design, evaluation, MCP, debugging the "context → action → observe" cycle.
- **Backend and distributed systems** — Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, queues, idempotency, observability.
- **Developer tooling** — Astro/Vite-based internal tools, CLI wrappers, team scripts.
- **DevOps and deploy automation** — Docker, GitHub Actions, GHCR, Dokploy, migrations on container startup.
